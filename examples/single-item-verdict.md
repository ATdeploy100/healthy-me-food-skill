# Example: single item

User sends a photo of a granola bar label: 12 g added sugar, canola oil third ingredient, 4 g protein.

**Verdict: Avoid.**
**Why:** 12 g added sugar and a seed oil in the top three ingredients, with almost no protein.
**Swap:** A plain Greek yogurt or a handful of almonds does the same job with 10 to 15 g protein and no added sugar.
