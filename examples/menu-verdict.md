# Example: menu verdict

User sends a photo of a diner breakfast menu, on their own, 8am.

**Order:** Veggie omelette with egg whites, side of smoked salmon, fruit instead of the home fries, black coffee.
**Why:** Protein around 35 g with no processed meat or refined starch on the plate.
**Backup:** Greek yogurt with berries, add a side of two eggs.
**Skip:**
- Pancake stack: refined flour and syrup
- Bacon and sausage platter: processed meat
- Orange juice: sugar without the fiber
**Habit note:** One coffee with the meal, none after your cutoff.
