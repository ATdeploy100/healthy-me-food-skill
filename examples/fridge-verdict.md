# Example: fridge verdict

User sends a photo of a fridge with eggs, spinach, Greek yogurt, leftover chicken, cheddar, a jar of salsa, tortillas, half an avocado, blueberries, a bottle of sweetened iced tea.

**Make:**
- Chicken and spinach scramble with salsa and avocado
- Greek yogurt with blueberries and whatever nuts are in the pantry
- Leftover chicken over spinach with avocado and a squeeze of lime
**Use first:** Spinach and avocado, both on the edge.
**Leave:**
- Tortillas: refined flour
- Cheddar: saturated fat, fine as a garnish, not the base
- Sweetened iced tea: sugar drink
**Missing:** A can of black beans or a bag of frozen broccoli would turn any of these into a full dinner.
