# Sources

Where the default numbers come from. The skill does not cite these in verdicts; they are here so you can check the defaults and change them if your clinician disagrees.

## Protein

- Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025 to 2030: 1.2 to 1.6 g protein per kg body weight per day. Harvard's reading of the guidelines, with the critique of source quality: https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/2026/01/09/dietary-guidelines-for-americans-2025-2030/
- Systematic review and meta-analysis of protein intake to support muscle mass and function in healthy adults (higher intakes, around 1.6 g/kg, for people training): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8978023/
- PROT-AGE Study Group position on protein for older adults (1.0 to 1.2 g/kg minimum, more when active or unwell): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23867520/
- Protein recommendations for muscle and bone in older adults, mini review (per-meal distribution, 25 to 40 g): https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2024.1394916/full

## Saturated fat, added sugar, alcohol

- Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025 to 2030: saturated fat under 10 percent of calories; no recommended amount of added sugar, practical ceiling about 10 g per meal; "consume less alcohol for better health" with no safe number given. Summary: https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/2026/01/09/dietary-guidelines-for-americans-2025-2030/ and https://www.hcplive.com/view/us-2025-2030-dietary-guidelines-addressing-alcohol-intake-recommendations

## Lipids and markers

- ACC/AHA lipid guideline, March 2026: ApoB for residual risk, Lp(a) measured once in adulthood with high at 125 nmol/L or 50 mg/dL, LDL-C thresholds under 100, 70 and 55 mg/dL by risk group: https://newsroom.heart.org/news/accaha-issue-updated-guideline-for-managing-lipids-cholesterol
- Common "optimal" targets for LDL, triglycerides, HDL, HbA1c, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, hs-CRP and HOMA-IR, as used by preventive and metabolic clinics: https://www.levels.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-understanding-your-cholesterol-panel-and-metabolic-blood-tests
- ApoB optimal ranges as used in preventive cardiology (under 80 mg/dL general, 60 to 70 at high risk): https://www.hcplive.com/view/expert-insights-lp-a-and-apob-in-the-2026-dyslipidemia-guidelines

## Caffeine

- Dose and timing effects of caffeine on sleep, randomized crossover trial (SLEEP, 2025): 100 mg up to four hours before bed had no significant effect; 400 mg disrupted sleep even twelve hours before bed: https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/48/4/zsae230/7815486
- Earlier trial on caffeine 0, 3 and 6 hours before bed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24235903/

## Supplements

- Stanford Lifestyle Medicine, supplements for healthy aging (creatine, omega-3, vitamin D, what the evidence supports): https://lifestylemedicine.stanford.edu/supplements-for-healthy-aging/
- Registered clinical trial of a protein, creatine, omega-3 and vitamin D combination in older adults, for the state of the evidence: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06920667

## Not included on purpose

Anything sold on a single small study, influencer protocols, and fasting windows. Add them to your own copy if you and your clinician want them.
