# Default food lists

Version 1.0.0, September 2026.

Generic defaults built on a whole-food, low-processed pattern. Anything the user puts in `inputs.md` overrides them. The eating pattern in `inputs.md` (vegan, kosher, halal, low-FODMAP, keto, allergies) is a hard filter applied before any of this; a filtered-out food never appears in an Order line, even as a compromise.

## Enjoy

- **Fish and seafood**: salmon, sardines, trout, anchovies, herring, mackerel, shrimp, scallops, cod. Low-mercury choices for anyone eating fish more than three times a week: skip swordfish, king mackerel, bigeye tuna, tilefish, and keep albacore to once a week.
- **Lean poultry**: chicken breast, turkey (skin off, not fried)
- **Eggs**
- **Plain dairy**: cottage cheese, plain Greek yogurt, kefir, skyr
- **Vegetables**: the more color and variety the better; aim for a different set across the week
- **Legumes**: lentils, chickpeas, black beans, edamame, hummus, tofu, tempeh
- **Nuts and seeds**: almonds, walnuts, pistachios, pumpkin seeds, chia, flax, hemp
- **Whole grains**: steel-cut oats, quinoa, brown or wild rice, barley, farro, whole-grain bread
- **Fruit**: berries, apples, pears, citrus, kiwi, avocado
- **Oils**: extra virgin olive oil, avocado oil
- **Fermented foods**: kimchi, sauerkraut, miso, kefir, plain yogurt
- **Herbs and spices**
- **Drinks**: water, sparkling water, herbal tea, green tea, black coffee

## Limit

- Red meat (lean cuts occasionally), full-fat cheese, butter, cream sauces
- Industrial seed oils in quantity (fried in canola, soybean, corn, sunflower, safflower, palm); a drizzle in a dressing is not the issue, deep frying is
- Added sugars and sweeteners of every kind; the practical ceiling is about 10 g added sugar per meal
- Refined grains: white rice, white bread, pasta, tortillas, pastries, crackers
- Processed snacks: chips, pretzels, granola bars
- Dried fruit and fruit juice
- Coconut oil and coconut cream
- Restaurant sauces and dressings served on the dish (sodium and sugar); on the side is the swap

## Avoid

- Deep-fried anything
- Processed and cured meats: bacon, sausage, hot dogs, deli meats, salami
- Sugar-sweetened drinks: soda, energy drinks, sweet tea, sweetened coffee drinks
- Sweet baked goods and candy
- Frozen and boxed convenience food with a long ingredient list
- Anything listing partially hydrogenated oil

## Tie-break ladder (default)

When two options are both Enjoy or both Limit, higher wins:

1. Fatty fish, grilled or baked, with vegetables
2. Legume-based dish with vegetables
3. Lean poultry with vegetables or whole grain
4. Egg or plain dairy dish with fruit, nuts or vegetables
5. Tofu or tempeh
6. Lean red meat, small portion, with vegetables
7. Anything with cream sauce, cheese crust or butter finish
8. Fried, breaded, processed meat, or sugar-based

Sides: vegetables or salad, then legumes, then whole grain, then potato, then white rice or bread, then fries.

### Ladder adjustments by goal or marker

- **Cholesterol, ApoB, LDL**: ladder as written. Red meat and butter-finished dishes drop one more rung.
- **Blood sugar, HbA1c, insulin**: whole grains move below legumes; potato moves below white rice in sides; fruit stays, juice and dried fruit go to Avoid.
- **Blood pressure**: soy-glazed, cured, brined and salt-crusted dishes drop a rung; potassium-rich sides (beans, greens, potato) move up.
- **Muscle, strength**: the protein rungs (1 to 6) are ranked by grams per portion when two are close; poultry and lean red meat rise a rung on training days.
- **Weight**: ladder as written; portion levers do the work (half the starch, skip the bread, one drink). Protein never shrinks.
- **High ferritin or iron saturation**: red meat drops to rung 7; fortified cereals and breads go to Avoid.
- **Plant-based**: tofu, tempeh and legumes are rungs 1 and 2; egg and dairy rows are removed for vegans.
- **Low-FODMAP, kosher, halal, allergies**: hard filter, then the ladder as written among what is left.

## Menu pattern shortcuts

- "Grilled", "baked", "roasted", "steamed", "poached", "seared": good signs
- "Crispy", "battered", "breaded", "tempura", "loaded", "smothered", "creamy", "glazed", "candied", "sticky": Limit or Avoid signs
- Standard asks: sauce on the side, vegetables or salad instead of fries, no bun or half the bread, grilled instead of fried, dressing on the side

## Default orders by cuisine (for Pre-scan when no menu is online)

- **American grill**: grilled salmon or chicken, vegetables instead of the potato, salad with dressing on the side
- **Italian**: grilled fish or chicken with vegetables, or a bean and vegetable soup to start; skip the bread basket, share the pasta if at all
- **Mexican**: bowl or fajitas with chicken or fish, black beans, salsa, guacamole, no rice or tortilla; skip the chips
- **Japanese**: sashimi or grilled fish, edamame, miso soup, seaweed salad; skip the tempura and the sauced rolls
- **Chinese or Thai**: steamed fish or chicken with vegetables, sauce on the side, brown rice if offered; skip the fried and the sweet-glazed dishes
- **Indian**: tandoori chicken or fish, dal, a vegetable dish, raita; skip the naan, the creamy kormas and the fried starters
- **Mediterranean or Middle Eastern**: grilled fish or chicken kebab, hummus, salad, grilled vegetables; skip the fried falafel and the pita basket
- **Steakhouse**: fish or the leanest cut, half portion or shared, vegetables and a salad; skip the creamed sides and the bread
- **Breakfast or diner**: eggs any style, add smoked salmon or cottage cheese, fruit; skip the pancakes, pastries and juice
- **Airline or hotel room service**: the protein plate (eggs, chicken, fish, yogurt), salad, water; skip the pasta and the dessert
- **Fast food**: grilled chicken sandwich, no mayo, side salad; or a burger with no bun and no fries
